Helpful Tips

Pre-Booking Preparation Checklist

A few quick details that help us design the perfect decor setup for your space.

Space Dimensions

Check the available wall width and ceiling clearance. Our standard circular rings measure 7 ft in height and need 8-10 ft width.

Power Outlet Access

If your package includes LED neon signs, fairy lights, or marquee numbers, ensure an electrical socket is available within 10-15 meters.

Theme & Color Inspo

Have 2-3 favorite color shades or an invitation card picture ready so we can match balloon and floral palettes seamlessly.

Venue Access Window

Confirm that your venue or apartment gives our team at least 2 to 3 hours of quiet setup access prior to guest arrival.
