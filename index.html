Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Everything you need to know about booking, setup timing, and customization.

How far in advance should I book my decoration?
We recommend booking at least 3 to 5 days in advance for weekends and peak celebration dates. However, we also accommodate same-day emergency and midnight surprise setups depending on decorator slot availability.
How long does the setup take on the day of the event?
Our standard Silver setups take approximately 1 hour, Blush ring arches take around 1.5 to 2 hours, and comprehensive Royal Platinum setups take 2.5 to 3.5 hours. Our stylists arrive early so everything is completed before your guests step in.
Can I customize the balloon colors and theme elements?
Yes, 100%! Every package can be customized. You can choose your color palette (e.g. pastel lilac, sage green, blush rose, chrome lavender) and add customized name cutouts or marquee numbers.
Do you provide props, neon lights, and metal arches on rent?
Yes! All metal frames, plinths, cake pedestals, and LED neon signs are included as rental equipment in our packages. Our team sets them up safely and collects them back respectfully after your event concludes.
Are the balloons safe for children and pets?
We exclusively use premium, natural biodegradable latex balloons that are non-toxic and odorless. We securely weight our installations so they remain steady throughout your celebration.
